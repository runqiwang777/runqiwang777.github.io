👋 Profile

My name is Runqi (Allen) Wang. I am an undergraduate student at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, pursuing a double major in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence.

During my undergraduate studies, I have conducted research under the guidance of Prof. Xutong Liu, Prof. Xuchuang Wang, and Prof. Shuai Li. I plan to join the PhD program in Statistics at SJTU in Fall 2027 under the supervision of Prof. Yiqing Lin. I am interested in work connecting rigorous theory with real-world applications, and I am always happy to exchange ideas.

🔍 Research Interests

My interests lie in machine learning, AI agents, and quantitative finance, especially theoretical decision-making and AI-driven quantitative research. On the theoretical side, I study sequential decisions such as multi-armed bandits and reinforcement learning. On the applied side, I explore statistical learning methods for financial modeling and data-driven investment decisions.
